Build a premium, modern, high-conversion corporate website for:

Construction Materials Trading & Distribution

TAGLINE:
“Quality Materials. Reliable Supply. Timely Delivery.”

IMPORTANT:
Use the uploaded SP BrickNova logo as the primary brand identity.
Do NOT redesign, alter, redraw, or replace the logo.
Use the exact visual identity from the logo:
- Deep brick red / construction red
- Charcoal / dark graphite
- White
- Subtle metallic grey accents

The website must feel like a premium construction-materials company, not a generic template, startup website, or AI-generated landing page.

==================================================
1. OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Create a strong, premium, industrial corporate aesthetic.

Design characteristics:
- Clean white backgrounds mixed with deep charcoal sections
- Brick-red accent color derived from the logo
- Large professional construction photography
- Strong typography
- Generous spacing
- Sharp geometric layouts
- Subtle shadows
- Minimal glass effects
- Premium micro-interactions
- Smooth scrolling
- Elegant reveal animations
- High-quality hover states
- Strong visual hierarchy

The visual language should communicate:

TRUST
RELIABILITY
QUALITY
SUPPLY CAPABILITY
PROFESSIONALISM
TIMELY DELIVERY

Avoid:
- Cartoon graphics
- Excessive gradients
- Neon colors
- Overuse of glassmorphism
- Generic stock-business layouts
- Excessive rounded cards
- Too many icons
- Fake statistics
- Fake testimonials
- Fake certifications
- Fake projects
- Fake client logos

Do not invent business information that has not been provided.
